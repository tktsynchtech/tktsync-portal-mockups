Meeting review · Updated 20 October 2026

Gaps identified. Designs updated.

The 40-screen HTML library reflects the meeting and subsequent scope decisions, including the simplified organizer partner flow. The layout, TktSync theme and four task groups are retained.

The event sequence stays the same:
TktSync onboarding and access → venue & seating chart → create event → publish offers → choose and connect ticketing partners → readiness and open sales.

Confirmed decisions and proposed design boundaries

  • Confirmed by you: TktSync onboards organizers; organizers select event partners after gaining access; own-event totals include partner breakdowns; error tracking is outside this portal.
  • Aligned in the meeting: enforce the customer-data terms agreed by organizer and ticketing partner.
  • Proposed in the meeting and now represented in the design: initial platform contact and public-event discovery. Agreement recording, data-sharing configuration and termination forms were subsequently removed from the organizer journey; commercial and legal policy is not finalized.
  • Still to decide: where verified agreement terms are recorded, final termination rules and any signing-provider integration. These are not extra steps in the organizer inquiry flow.

Outside this portal change

Error logs, API failure diagnostics and transaction-error investigation are excluded. Complex seating-chart authoring remains in its existing tool. Ticketing companies retain payment collection, refunds and settlement.

These are interactive HTML mockups, with illustrative data and fees. CSV filtering and approval gates demonstrate intended behavior; server authorization, storage, integrations and deployed acceptance have not been implemented or proven by this design update. Original affected screens and earlier browser edits are backed up before migration.
